Support & Service Level Agreement

Applies to: Edit Inheritance for Confluence (Confluence Cloud, Atlassian Forge).

How to get support

Email support@parviz.tj. Please include your Confluence site URL, the page where it happens, what you expected and what happened, and screenshots if you have them. Never send passwords or API tokens.

Support hours and response time

Item Commitment
Support hours Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM US Eastern Time (New York)
First response within 24 hours on business days

Bugs and security issues

  • Bugs are confirmed, prioritised and fixed in a new app version. Forge apps update automatically for all customers.
  • Security vulnerabilities: report them to support@parviz.tj with "SECURITY" in the subject. They are fixed within the timeframes of the Atlassian Marketplace Security Bug Fix Policy.
  • Security incidents that affect customers are reported to Atlassian and affected customers.

Availability

The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge ("Runs on Atlassian") and has no servers of its own, so its availability follows the Atlassian Cloud platform. See Atlassian status.

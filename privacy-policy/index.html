Privacy Policy — Edit Inheritance for Confluence

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Edit Inheritance for Confluence ("the app") is provided by Parviz Kandiyorov ("we"). This policy explains what data the app processes when installed on an Atlassian Confluence Cloud site.

Where the app runs

The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge infrastructure ("Runs on Atlassian"). It has no external servers and sends no data outside Atlassian. We, the vendor, have no access to your Confluence content or to the data the app stores.

Data the app stores

Inside Atlassian Forge storage for your site only: - the IDs of pages where inheritance is turned on, and the time it was turned on; - progress counters of "apply to existing child pages" jobs. No page content, titles, user names, email addresses or other personal data are stored.

Data the app processes but does not store

To do its job the app reads, through Confluence APIs: page IDs and the page hierarchy, page edit and view restrictions (Atlassian account IDs and group IDs), and the current user's permissions. User and group names are shown in the app dialog to the user who opens it and are not stored.

Logs

Atlassian Forge keeps function logs per its retention policy. Our logs contain only page IDs, event types, counts, HTTP status codes and timings — no names, titles or email addresses.

Data retention and deletion

Stored data is deleted when inheritance is turned off for a page or when the app is uninstalled.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data. There are no sub-processors other than Atlassian.

Your rights

Because the app stores no personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct or delete. For questions contact support@parviz.tj.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes.
